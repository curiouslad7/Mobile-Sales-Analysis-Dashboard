# 📱 Mobile Sales Analysis — Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Mobile Sales Analysis Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard is designed to analyze mobile sales performance across different dimensions such as **mobile model, brand, payment method, month, city, customer ratings, and day of the week**.

The main objective of the project is to transform raw mobile sales data into an **interactive and visually appealing business intelligence dashboard** that makes it easier to identify sales trends and customer behavior.

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

The dashboard provides insights into:

* Overall sales performance
* Total quantity of mobile phones sold
* Number of transactions
* Average sales value
* Sales performance across different cities
* Monthly sales quantity trends
* Customer rating distribution
* Payment method preferences
* Brand-wise sales and quantity
* Mobile model performance
* Sales performance by day of the week

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 🗺️ Total Sales by City

A geographical visualization is used to understand the distribution of mobile sales across different cities in India.

This helps identify locations with higher sales activity and provides a geographical perspective of the business performance.

### 📅 Total Quantity by Month

The monthly trend chart shows how the quantity of mobile phones sold changes throughout the year.

It helps identify:

* High-performing months
* Low-performing months
* Overall sales trends
* Changes in demand over time

### ⭐ Customer Ratings

The customer rating visualization shows the distribution of ratings from **1 to 5**.

This provides an overview of customer feedback and helps understand the relationship between sales and customer satisfaction.

### 💳 Transactions by Payment Method

The dashboard analyzes transactions across different payment methods, including:

* UPI
* Debit Card
* Cash
* Credit Card

This helps understand customer payment preferences.

### 🏷️ Brand Analysis

A detailed table compares mobile brands based on:

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity
* Transactions

The dashboard includes brands such as **Apple, OnePlus, Samsung, Vivo, and Xiaomi**.

### 📱 Sales by Mobile Model

The mobile model visualization highlights models generating higher sales.

This allows users to identify the best-performing models and compare their contribution to overall sales.

### 📆 Sales by Day Name

The dashboard also analyzes sales performance across different days of the week.

This can help identify patterns in customer purchasing behavior throughout the week.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to filter the analysis based on:

* **Mobile Model**
* **Payment Method**
* **Brand**
* **Day Name**
* **Month**

The month navigation on the left side also allows users to explore sales performance for individual months.

These interactive filters make it possible to move from a high-level overview to more specific analysis.
